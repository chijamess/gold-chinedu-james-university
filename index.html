About us

Built to produce graduates who can work on day one.

Gold Chinedu-James University was founded on a simple complaint: too many graduates leave school knowing the theory and nothing about how the work actually gets done. We built our programmes backward from what employers told us they needed.

Today the university runs 38 programmes across engineering, computing, business and the sciences, taught by faculty who still consult and build in their fields.

Our mission

To give every student a degree that opens doors immediately, not eventually.

Our values

Rigour over shortcuts. Practice over theory alone. Community over competition.
